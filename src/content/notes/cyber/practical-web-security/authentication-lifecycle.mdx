## Authentication is a lifecycle

A sign-in form is only one step in authentication. The surrounding system also needs to handle session creation, renewal, recovery, and revocation consistently.

Review the complete journey from credential submission through logout and account recovery. A secure step can be undermined when another step leaves stale access behind.
